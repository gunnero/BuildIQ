# V1 Modules

BuildIQ V1 contains the modules listed below.

## 1. Authentication

Users log in with credentials and receive JWT authentication for API access.

## 2. Companies

Companies own users, customers, projects, calculations, estimates, payments, and expenses.

## 3. Users / Employees

Employees belong to a company and perform project, estimate, payment, and expense workflows.

## 4. Customers

Customers store the people or organizations receiving construction work and offers.

## 5. Projects

Projects belong to customers and store agreed price, project status, payment status, totals, and related rooms.

## 6. Rooms

Rooms belong to projects and group measurements and calculations by physical space.

## 7. Measurements

Measurements store dimensions such as length, width, height, area, perimeter, openings, and quantity inputs.

## 8. Painting Calculator

Calculates wall and ceiling painting areas, paint quantity, primer quantity, labor, and material list items.

## 9. Tile Calculator

Calculates tile area, tile quantity, adhesive, grout, waste percentage, labor, and material list items.

## 10. Knauf Calculator

Calculates gypsum board, profiles, screws, jointing material, insulation, labor, and material list items.

## 11. Flooring Calculator

Calculates floor area, flooring material, underlay, skirting, waste percentage, labor, and material list items.

## 12. Material List

Combines calculated materials into a project-level list that can be reviewed before an estimate is generated.

## 13. Estimates / Offers

Creates project offers with customer information, room scope, material items, labor items, totals, notes, and payment terms.

## 14. Payments

Stores payment history and calculates total paid, remaining balance, and payment status.

## 15. Expenses

Stores project-related costs such as materials, subcontractors, transport, and other expenses.

## 16. Dashboard

Shows project, customer, payment, expense, and outstanding balance summaries.

## 17. PDF Quote Generation

Generates Macedonian PDF offers/quotes from approved estimate data.

## Core V1 Workflow

1. Log in.
2. Create a customer.
3. Create a project for that customer.
4. Add one or more rooms.
5. Enter room dimensions.
6. Run construction calculations.
7. Generate an estimate.
8. Record payments.
9. Review total paid.
10. Review remaining balance.
11. Generate a Macedonian PDF offer/quote.
